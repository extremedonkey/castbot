# :rowboat: Introducing the Marooning Planner
-# Build your tribes in secret. Reveal them when you're ready.

You've read every application. You've scored them, argued about them, and finally locked your cast. :tada:

Then comes the part nobody talks about: **working out who goes on which tribe.**

Right now that probably lives in a spreadsheet, a group DM, and somebody's notes app. You're cross-referencing timezones against a castlist in another tab, guessing at ages from memory, and quietly praying nobody in production accidentally hands out a tribe role three days early.

**CastBot does that part now.** :sparkles:

## :thought_balloon: Draft tribes that nobody can see
Open :rowboat: **Marooning**, hit :thought_balloon: **Draft Tribes**, and drop your cast into tribes. Move people around as much as you like. Try the returnees split up. Try them together. Change your mind at 2am.

**Nothing leaves CastBot.** No Discord roles are assigned. No channels are created. Nobody is pinged. Your spectators, your cast, and that one player who checks the member list every ten minutes see **exactly nothing**.

> The only moment a tribe becomes public is when *you* add it to a castlist. That's it. That's the reveal.

## :bar_chart: Balance them properly, at a glance
Every player renders on one line with the things you actually balance around:

```1. Internet Crybaby - 24yo | @She/Her | @AEST```

Age, pronouns and timezone, right there next to the name — no second tab, no cross-referencing. Each tribe shows its own size, so you can see a 5–3 split forming before you commit to it.

Got a stacked tribe? Move someone. Timezone dead zone? You'll spot it.

## :warning: Never chase a confirmation again
Here's the one that quietly saves seasons. CastBot flags **every player who still needs something from you**:

> :warning::black_nib: **No offer sent** — they don't know they're in yet
> :warning::incoming_envelope: **No reply** — you told them, they haven't answered
> :warning::no_entry_sign: **Declined** — time to go to your alternates

Players who've **accepted are completely unmarked**. So a clean tribe with no warning icons means everyone in it is locked in — and anything still outstanding jumps off the screen.

No more marooning day discovering that one person never actually confirmed. :sweat_smile:

## :busts_in_silhouette: Late additions, sorted
Someone dropped out and you're bringing in a replacement who never applied? Recruited off Discord — Instagram, an IRL league, a friend of a friend?

:busts_in_silhouette: **Add Cast** builds their application channel for you, exactly as if they'd clicked Apply themselves. They then flow through :trophy: Casting and :rowboat: Marooning like everyone else. No special cases, no manual channel wrangling.
