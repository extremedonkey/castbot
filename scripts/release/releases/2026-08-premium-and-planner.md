-# Sunday 9th August 2026

The big one: **CastBot Premium** is now self-serve, Season Planner learned about **reward challenges**, and the Marooning tab has been rebuilt around the thing you actually use it for. :tada:

## :star: CastBot Premium
Premium is out of the allowlist and open to everyone. Support CastBot on Ko-fi and the full host toolkit unlocks for your server — :space_invader: Ask CastBot, bulk channel tools, Safari power ops, and early access to whatever lands next.
> Usage: `/menu` → :star: **Get Premium** → :tickets: **Redeem** → enter your Ko-fi email

**You redeem it yourself.** Enter the email on your Ko-fi account and Premium activates on the spot — no waiting on anyone to flip a switch.

**One subscription, one server, moveable.** New season, new server? Tap :tickets: Redeem in the *new* server and CastBot offers to move it across for you. No cooldown — shift it back and forth as often as you like.

## :calendar_spiral: Challenge Days & Reward Challenges
Season Planner rounds now take a **challenge length**, so a round isn't locked to a single day of competition.
> Usage: `/menu` → :calendar_spiral: Season Manager → :calendar_spiral: **Planner** → open a round

You can also link a **bonus / reward challenge** to a round and place it before, same-day, or after the main one. It **shares the round's day budget** — so adding a reward never quietly stretches your season out.

The :bar_chart: Schedule grows a column for it, and the :calendar_spiral: Calendar paints reward days in pink.

## :rowboat: The Marooning Planner
The Marooning tab has been rebuilt. **Casting decisions now lead**, tribes sit underneath, and the stats summary is gone — every number in it was already on screen.

Each player is now **one line**: `2. ReeceBot - 27yo | @Ask | @CST / CDT`. Scores and vote counts moved off the roster (by marooning you've already decided — and they're one click away on :trophy: Casting), which halves the height of a long cast list.

Cast Players and Alternate no longer split into three sub-sections. It's **one list per section, grouped by draft tribe**, with a player count on every tribe, and only *outstanding work* flagged:
> :warning::black_nib: no offer sent yet
> :warning::incoming_envelope: offer sent, no reply
> :warning::no_entry_sign: declined

Accepted players carry **no marker at all** — if everything is flagged, nothing stands out.

## :busts_in_silhouette: Add Cast
Someone joining who never applied through CastBot — a late addition, or a recruit you found off-Discord?
> Usage: `/menu` → :calendar_spiral: Season Manager → :rowboat: Marooning → :busts_in_silhouette: **Add Cast**

Pick them from a list and CastBot creates their application channel **exactly as if they'd clicked Apply themselves** — same permissions, same welcome message. They then flow through :trophy: Casting and :rowboat: Marooning like anyone else. Anyone who already has an application is skipped, never duplicated.

## :compass: A Simpler Menu
**Tools is gone.** Everything it held moved to where you'd actually look for it — Vanity into Settings, Import/Export into Map Explorer, Timers into Utilities as :stopwatch: Stopwatch and :snowflake: Snowflake.

Map Explorer gained its own :tools: **Utilities** submenu, there's a written **user guide**, and the main menu now carries a **Support Server** link.

## :wrench: Notable Fixes
**Casting offers.** A production member clicking an applicant's placement card used to *destroy* it — dismissing the public message and leaving the applicant unable to accept. Now only the applicant can act on it; anyone else gets a private note.

**Season Planner estimates.** Editing your apply button quietly erased your planner estimates — players, swaps, FTC, start date — while leaving the rounds behind, so the Planner claimed it wasn't set up yet. Fixed, and the estimates are safe from every other edit too.

**Draft Tribes never leaked, but New Tribe did.** Creating a tribe from the Marooning tab assigned real Discord roles and published it to your castlist. From Marooning it now creates the role and nothing else — your casting plans stay private until you publish them.
